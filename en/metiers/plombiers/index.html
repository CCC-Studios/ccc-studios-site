Service 1

Your Google profile, set up for plumbing

It’s often where customers see you first, before your website. Here’s what we check and fix.

The category

“Plumber” as the main category, then the right secondary ones: heating, water heaters, drain cleaning. It’s the field that decides which searches you show up in.

Your services, one by one

Water heaters, heat pumps, backwater valves, frozen pipes, bathroom renovations. Customers search for a specific problem: your profile has to name it.

Your real hours

Show “24 hours” only if someone really answers. An unanswered call during your listed hours costs more than shorter hours.

Your area

The towns you really travel to, the same list on Google and on your site. Your address can stay hidden.

Your licences

Your RBQ licence number on the profile and the site, and your CMMTQ membership if you’re a member. Customers check, especially for a big job.

Your reviews

A short link sent by text after every service call, and a reply to every review. Never bought, traded or self-written reviews.

Your photos

Photos of real jobs: an installed water heater, a clean mechanical room, the truck. Never AI-made images on a Google profile.

The call button

We test it ourselves, on a phone. One wrong digit, and every emergency call goes somewhere else.
