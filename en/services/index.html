Questions

Why start with the Google profile?

Because that's often where customers find you first, before your site or social media. A wrong category or wrong contact details can cost calls without anyone noticing.

Will it look AI-made?

What we show of your work is your real photos. The voice is synthetic, and the video says so. If you prefer a presenter on screen, Jennifer is an option, always declared as AI. A person writes every script and chooses every image.

Do I have to take several services?

No. Each service stands alone, once or monthly. The one rule: a video comes with service B, so your social accounts are ready for it.

Does a Google profile cost money?

No. A Google Business Profile is free: Google doesn’t sell it. What you pay for is our work. You stay the owner of your profile; we’re only a manager, and you can remove us at any time. Google explains what to know before working with an agency on this page.

How does payment work?

For a one-time service: half to start, half on acceptance. For a monthly service: paid in advance on the 1st of each month. By Interac e-Transfer.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes. Dan handles calls and meetings in French, English or Spanish, and your posts can have a Spanish version.

What about paid ads?

That's service C. We do the plan and the tracking, once your accounts are in order. The budget goes straight from you to the platform, in your own accounts; it never passes through us.

Is optimization only about AI?

No. We start with AI because it's our specialty, but the method comes from industrial engineering: we improve the process first, then pick the tool. Sometimes a simple formula does better than AI, and we'll tell you so.
