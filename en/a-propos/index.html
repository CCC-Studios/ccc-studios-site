About CCC Studios

Everything can be improved. We prove it.

CCC Studios is the Canadian Creative Company: a Quebec studio co-founded by Dan Ortega and Emilien Spriet, where creativity and optimization work together.

The studio

Two branches, one way of working: understand, measure, then improve.

Business branchBe seen, be chosen, run better: for Quebec service businesses, with Jennifer, our AI actress. Creative branchThe World of Croussus: worlds, series, games and stories written by people, brought to images by AI.

The co-founders

Dan Ortega

Industrial Engineering BEng graduate, co-op program, Concordia University, Gina Cody School of Engineering and Computer Science, 2021.

At Airbus, he spent three years improving the A220 final assembly line in Mirabel. Today he brings the same method to local businesses: watch a process, measure it, remove what serves no purpose, automate the rest, then measure again.

He works in French, English and Spanish. His passion for aviation comes from the Air Cadets, where he was the cadet commanding officer of a 115-cadet squadron.

Emilien Spriet

A seasoned businessman and salesman, Emilien runs his own rental agency. He has experience in construction, renovation, carpentry, condominium management and rental building management: he knows from the inside the daily life of the contractors and property managers we serve.

He has composed multi-instrumental tracks since childhood, from hip-hop to cinematic, orchestral and solo piano music, and he co-created the World of Croussus, CCC's fantasy universe, with Dan.

Our method, from factory to office

  1. ObserveWe look at how the work really gets done, not how it should.
  2. MeasureTime, errors, delays: an honest starting point.
  3. SimplifyWe remove the steps that serve no purpose before adding any tool.
  4. Equip, then measure againAI and automation do the rest, and the numbers tell whether it works.
